# 01-ingest-approved-decision

## Purpose

Collect already-approved strategy, audit decisions, copy, evidence and project context.

## Inputs

- CRO audit/decision
- approved copy
- PRD/spec
- Measurement Contract
- CRM Data Contract
- repo context

## Process

1. Read the conversation/spec/audit instead of re-interviewing.
2. List approved decisions and exact sources.
3. Preserve upstream vocabulary.
4. Flag contradictions.

## Outputs

- decision inventory
- approval/evidence status

## Test

Do not ask a fresh strategic interview when the current conversation already settles the decision.

# 05-audit-form-and-friction

## Purpose

Evaluate form placement, field justification, step count, quiz/booking friction and downstream quality impact.

## Inputs

- form evidence
- CRM/funnel data when available

## Process

1. Map every step and field to qualification/trust/conversion/attribution/operations/compliance value.
2. Compare that value with abandonment/cognitive/technical cost.
3. Protect justified friction.
4. Route implementation separately.
5. Use `references/coo-cro-principles.md` to protect useful friction and `references/masterclass-cro-heuristics.md` when evaluating form placement or unnecessary conversion steps.

## Outputs

- friction map
- KEEP/PROTECT/SIMPLIFY/MERGE/REMOVE/TEST decisions

## Test

A qualification field that lowers CVR but improves qualified-lead rate must be eligible for PROTECT, not automatically removed.

# 06-prioritize-opportunities

## Purpose

Rank only the highest-leverage changes using business impact, confidence, evidence and effort.

## Inputs

- all audit findings

## Process

1. Rank by business impact, evidence confidence, reversibility and effort.
2. Keep the list small.
3. Exclude changes blocked by missing evidence unless framed as evidence-gathering.
4. Preserve CRM quality as a guardrail.
5. Use `references/masterclass-cro-heuristics.md` only as a source of candidate mechanisms; evidence and business impact determine priority.

## Outputs

- ranked 1-3 opportunities
- reason
- blocked dependencies

## Test

Given ten medium issues and one blocker, prioritize the blocker and a small number of next changes rather than a full redesign.

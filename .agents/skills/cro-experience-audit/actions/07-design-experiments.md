# 07-design-experiments

## Purpose

Turn eligible opportunities into bounded experiments with measurement and decision rules.

## Inputs

- prioritized opportunities
- measurement context

## Process

1. Define the hypothesis and variable.
2. Define the expected front-end and downstream signal.
3. Define minimum data and decision rule.
4. Define risk/rollback and owner.
5. Read `references/experiment-principles.md` and `references/masterclass-cro-heuristics.md` before finalizing the test.

## Outputs

- 1-3 experiment briefs

## Test

Every experiment must identify hypothesis, variable, metric, downstream signal when required, minimum data and decision rule.

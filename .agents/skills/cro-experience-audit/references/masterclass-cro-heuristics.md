# CRO masterclass heuristics

These are testable heuristics distilled from the supplied video, transcript, written synthesis and slide deck. They are not universal requirements.

## High-leverage page heuristics

Evaluate whether the current context would benefit from:
- a form embedded directly in the landing experience rather than an unnecessary intermediate contact page;
- fewer escape routes on tightly focused paid-acquisition pages;
- a clear benefit-led hero and direct CTA;
- strong proof close to claims and conversion points;
- video testimonials or high-quality case evidence when authentic assets exist;
- a short founder/expert video when trust and explanation are conversion constraints;
- explicit objection handling and risk reversal when supported by the offer;
- mobile action accessibility such as a sticky CTA when it improves usability;
- fast loading and mobile-first execution;
- a clear thank-you / next-step path.

Never invent testimonials, logos, results, guarantees or proof assets to satisfy a heuristic.

## Copy and scanning

Assume many visitors scan. Prioritize critical persuasive content in:
- H1/subhead;
- bullets;
- proof captions;
- CTA copy;
- objection answers.

Prefer concrete benefits and outcome clarity over feature lists when the evidence supports the claims.

## Search-intent variants

For search-led acquisition, consider route variants keyed by relevant dimensions such as:
- service/programme;
- location;
- audience/context;
- acquisition source.

Only create variants when they can be genuinely differentiated and useful. Preserve message continuity through:

`query → ad → page/H1 → form expectation → thank-you/follow-up → sales reality`

Do not mass-produce thin or near-duplicate pages merely because a matrix is technically possible.

## Testing and behavioral evidence

Use behavioral and experiment evidence to validate heuristics:
- controlled A/B or route tests when appropriate;
- persistent assignment when required by the experiment design;
- session recordings / heatmaps as diagnostic evidence, not causal proof;
- conversion plus downstream quality guardrails;
- winner becomes the new control only after the agreed decision rule is met.

The source explicitly warns that no checklist guarantees a target conversion rate. Treat the data as the deciding evidence.

## Speed-to-lead boundary

Fast follow-up can be a high-leverage operational hypothesis after form submission, but activation ownership is separate from page CRO. Route implementation to the activation/CRM owner when the issue is response SLA, lead routing, call bridge or follow-up automation.

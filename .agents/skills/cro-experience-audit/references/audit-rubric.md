# CRO audit rubric

Score evidence, not aesthetics.

Audit dimensions:
1. traffic/intent fit;
2. message match;
3. offer clarity;
4. conversion action fit;
5. above-the-fold clarity;
6. form access and justified friction;
7. proof quality and proximity;
8. objections and risk reversal;
9. mobile usability;
10. page performance/clarity;
11. tracking readiness;
12. CRM/downstream quality.

For every issue record:
- observation;
- evidence;
- severity: `BLOCKER | HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW`;
- mechanism: why it may hurt conversion or quality;
- recommended change;
- test hypothesis;
- measurement requirement;
- owner/handoff.

Never invent testimonials, accreditations, rankings, logos, figures, or conversion benchmarks.

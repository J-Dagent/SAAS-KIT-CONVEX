# Experiment principles

A CRO test requires:
- hypothesis;
- one primary variable unless explicitly a route test;
- primary front-end metric;
- downstream CRM/business signal when available;
- minimum data condition;
- decision horizon;
- rollback;
- owner.

Priority order:
1. route/offer mismatch;
2. message match and above-fold clarity;
3. CTA/form friction;
4. proof;
5. objections;
6. presentation polish.

Recommend at most 1–3 next experiments. Do not call a page a winner from LP conversion alone when CRM quality is available or required.
